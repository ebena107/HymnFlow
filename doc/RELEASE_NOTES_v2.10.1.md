# HymnFlow v2.10.1 — BibliaTodo Attribution Update

**Release date**: 2026-11-26

A small follow-up to v2.10.0 that applies the publisher's confirmed attribution details.

---

## 📖 BibliaTodo credits

- The **Licensing & credits** panel now shows BibliaTodo's current credit wording for NTBT and BTNT, with the `bibliatodo.org` page and license links.
- Documentation (README, setup guide, setup page and [CONTENT_LICENSING](https://github.com/ebena107/HymnFlow/blob/master/doc/CONTENT_LICENSING.md)) cites the current agreement, **version 1.9**, and explains that one general credit in your documentation or app information is enough — you do not need to repeat it beside every verse on the overlay.
- Printing a complete Bible or Testament for distribution needs the publisher's free registration and approval; that does not apply to importing or displaying Scripture in HymnFlow.

No other behavior changes. **Upgrade**: replace the plugin files; your hymns, services, settings and Bibles are untouched. Full details: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/ebena107/HymnFlow/blob/master/doc/CHANGELOG.md).
